IMPLEMENTED MILESTONE
Official TREC 2021 acquisition/provenance, streaming XML loader, full data profile,
disk-backed BM25 search, and evaluation on the fixed first 10 development topics.
The index covers all 375,580 trials. Raw XML is preserved with normalized fields.
The baseline measures lexical relevance; it does not establish eligibility.
