BM25 V1 - ENGINEERING ERROR ANALYSIS

Scope: full 375,580-trial snapshot, first ten TREC 2021 development patients.
Aggregate result: nDCG@10 0.4112, eligible recall@10 1.93%, recall@30 4.92%,
and recall@200 18.35% (macro averages across ten topics). Across the 100 top-ten
positions, 16 are judged eligible, 45 excluded, 35 irrelevant and four unjudged.

This report inspects benchmark labels and source text; it does not independently
adjudicate clinical eligibility. Pooled judgments are incomplete.

1. TOPICAL RELEVANCE DOES NOT ESTABLISH ELIGIBILITY
For topic 1 (spinal anaplastic astrocytoma after several therapies), the top ten
contain zero grade-2 eligible trials and seven grade-1 excluded trials. The first
two titles mention anaplastic astrocytoma, so disease words alone are insufficient.
BM25 does not reason about tumor location, treatment history or exclusion rules.
The benchmark labels establish the exclusion counts; the precise clinical reason
for each exclusion is not established by the label alone.

2. LONG NOTES CAN MATCH SECONDARY FEATURES
Topic 1 ranks NCT03784729, an acupuncture study for intermittent claudication due
to lumbar spinal stenosis, third. The patient note contains spinal disease,
weakness, walking difficulty and chronic pain, while its primary tumor diagnosis
is different. This illustrates secondary-symptom overlap. It suggests testing
disease-focused query extraction; token-level attribution has not been measured.

3. ELIGIBLE TRIALS ARE LOST BEFORE ANY RERANKER COULD HELP
For topic 1, only five of 47 judged eligible trials appear in the top 200.
Misses include NCT00002814 and NCT00003470, both of whose titles explicitly name
anaplastic astrocytoma. These are known benchmark positives, not new medical
eligibility conclusions. A reranker restricted to 200 cannot recover these misses.

For topic 10 (systemic mastocytosis), only one of 11 judged eligible trials appears
in the top 200 and none appears in the top ten. Misses include NCT00001356
(Culture and Characteristics of Mastocytosis Mast Cells) and NCT00044122
(Regulation of the Proliferation and Survival of Normal and Neoplastic Human Mast
Cells). Their conditions include mastocytosis. The note also includes numerous
medications, acute symptoms and discharge details. Query dilution is a hypothesis
to test, not a demonstrated causal explanation for every miss.

4. NEXT CONTROLLED EXPERIMENTS
- Freeze this run and retain its rankings and metrics as the baseline.
- Compare disease-focused queries with whole-note queries on a separately chosen
  TREC 2021 development/validation split. Record clinical abbreviations and negation.
- Add biomedical dense retrieval and reciprocal rank fusion; first measure eligible
  recall@200 to determine whether additional positives reach the candidate pool.
- Then test conservative age/sex filters with unknown retained, followed by
  criterion-level reranking for eligible-versus-excluded separation.
- Keep TREC 2022 untouched until settings and evaluation conventions are frozen.

The native FTS5 top-k optimization preserves rankings and scores. It is a runtime
implementation change, not a relevance improvement. reports/validation.json records
exact comparison with the earlier exhaustive implementation for topics 1 and 10.
The complete ten-topic aggregate and per-topic results are in eval.md/metrics.json;
error_cases.json contains notes, actual query tokens, top-ten labels and eligible
miss examples for all ten patients.
