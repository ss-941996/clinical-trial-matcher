TREC 2021: REVIEW OF THE FIRST 10 DEVELOPMENT TOPICS
Reviewed on 16 November 2026 from the official downloaded patient topics.
This is a data/engineering review, not a medical eligibility assessment.

Topic 1: Spinal anaplastic astrocytoma with prior radiation and multiple systemic
therapies. Preserve tumor location and treatment history; a broad brain-tumor
keyword match does not establish eligibility. Judged eligible trials: 47.

Topic 2: Aortic stenosis with a bicuspid valve and reduced ventricular function.
Contains many abbreviations and shifted date placeholders. Do not mistake the
placeholder year for patient age or infer current recruitment. Eligible: 138.

Topic 3: Intraventricular hemorrhage and an arteriovenous malformation. Narrative
mentions an initial injury but subsequent investigation changes the clinical
focus. Full-note retrieval will include both contexts. Eligible: 32.

Topic 4: Burkitt lymphoma presenting in the heart, with negative marrow/CSF
involvement and a long past history. Negated involvement and historical events
must remain distinct from present disease. Eligible: 44.

Topic 5: Bilateral carotid stenosis amid substantial comorbidity. Includes symptom
negations and explicit numerical stenosis measurements. Broad cardiovascular
terms may dominate a naive retrieval query. Eligible: 119.

Topic 6: Recurrent C. difficile infection and dialysis-dependent renal disease.
Contains medication aliases, abbreviations and a treatment timeline. These are
potentially important to later eligibility checks. Eligible: 117.

Topic 7: HCV cirrhosis and hepatic encephalopathy; prior interferon nonresponse.
A documented negative workup for stroke must not become a positive diagnosis.
Historical alcohol use and current abstinence require temporal context. Eligible: 125.

Topic 8: CLL with Richter transformation after multiple treatment regimens.
Treatment response and order matter; broad leukemia relevance alone is not a
sufficient match to a trial's required disease subtype. Eligible: 92.

Topic 9: Epilepsy with intellectual disability and a detailed medication history.
Only 13 eligible versus 201 excluded judgments. This is a useful later test of
whether criterion checking demotes relevant-but-excluded trials.

Topic 10: Systemic mastocytosis, repeated flares and elevated tryptase.
Only 11 eligible judgments. Inspect rare-condition retrieval failures carefully;
a high recall gain/loss can be driven by a small number of records.

BASELINE DECISIONS
Use the unmodified note as the retrieval input, with only deterministic lexical
tokenization and a fixed English stopword list. Do not tune on these examples
before recording the first baseline. Keep negations and date placeholders in the
source; this baseline does not claim to reason about either. No LLM calls.

The first 10 topics are a development slice. Their results cannot be described
as performance on all 75 topics or on a held-out year. TREC 2022 remains untouched.
